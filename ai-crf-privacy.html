Privacy Policy for AI CRF

Last Updated: 2026

AI CRF is a browser extension built to help users simplify input operations. This privacy statement describes how we handle data and explains the purpose of each required permission.

Core Statement

We do NOT collect, upload, share or store your personal information. All temporary data generated while using this extension will be cleared when you close or log out of the tab.

Data Retention

Temporary working data only exists locally in your browser session. Once you log out or close the tab, all related temporary data will be deleted automatically. No data will be sent to our remote servers.
